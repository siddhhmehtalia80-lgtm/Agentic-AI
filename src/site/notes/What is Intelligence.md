## What is Intelligence Termed as in General?

In general terms, **intelligence** is broadly defined as the overall ability to acquire, process, understand, and apply knowledge and skills. It combines several core cognitive capabilities:

- **Reasoning & Problem-Solving:** Using logic to draw conclusions and navigate new situations.
    
- **Adaptability:** Learning from past experiences and adjusting behavior when environments change.
    
- **Abstract Thought:** Understanding complex, non-physical ideas, structures, and patterns.
    
- **Comprehension:** Grasping language, subtle context, and relationships between concepts.
  
  In Artificial Intelligence, **intelligence** is generally defined as the ability of an artificial system to perceive its environment, process information, learn from experience, make decisions, and take actions to achieve specific goals.

Unlike human intelligence, which encompasses emotion, consciousness, and physical experience, AI intelligence is evaluated through computational capability and problem-solving performance.

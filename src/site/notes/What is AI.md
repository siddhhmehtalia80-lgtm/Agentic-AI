## What is AI?

see:[[About\|About]]
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating machines and software capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence. These tasks include learning from data, solving problems, understanding language, recognizing images, making decisions, and generating content.

Examples of AI:

- ChatGPT
- Google Gemini
  # How Does AI Work?

AI works by learning patterns from large amounts of data instead of being explicitly programmed for every task.

### Basic Working Process

1. **Data Collection**
    - AI is trained using massive datasets such as text, images, videos, and audio.
2. **Training**
    - Machine Learning algorithms analyze the data to identify patterns and relationships.
3. **Model Creation**
    - The AI creates a mathematical model based on what it has learned.
4. **Prediction or Decision Making**
    - When given a new input, the trained model predicts or generates the most suitable output.
5. **Continuous Improvement**
    - Some AI systems improve over time by learning from new data and user interactions.
      
  
  # What is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI is an advanced type of Artificial Intelligence that can plan, reason, make decisions, and complete tasks autonomously with minimal human intervention.

Unlike traditional AI, which responds to individual prompts, Agentic AI can:

- Break complex tasks into smaller steps.
- Plan the best sequence of actions.
- Use different tools and APIs.
- Access external information when required.
- Remember previous context during a task.
- Execute multi-step workflows automatically.
  
  **Key Definitions and Angles in AI**

- **Rational Action (The Standard Definition):** Modern AI focuses heavily on the concept of a "rational agent"—a system that observes its environment and takes the best possible action to maximize its chance of success or achieve a target goal.
    
- **Pattern Recognition & Learning:** From a machine learning perspective, intelligence is the capacity to recognize complex patterns in vast amounts of data and generalize that knowledge to handle new, unseen situations without explicit step-by-step programming.
    
- **Adaptability:** The ability to adjust strategies, self-correct errors, and optimize performance when the environment or inputs change over time.
    

**The Two Main Categories of AI Intelligence**

- **Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI):** Intelligence focused on a single, specific task (e.g., playing chess, translating languages, or recognizing facial features). It can outperform humans in its specific domain but lacks general reasoning.
    
- **Artificial General Intelligence (AGI):** A hypothetical level of intelligence where a machine can learn, understand, adapt, and apply knowledge across any intellectual task at or above a human level.

## Obsidian is a free, markdown-based note-taking and knowledge-management app. Unlike traditional cloud-based note apps, Obsidian stores all files directly on your device as local text files (`.md`), giving you complete ownership and offline access to your data.

Its defining feature is **bi-directional linking**, which allows you to link notes together to build a personal network of interconnected ideas (often called a "digital brain" or _Zettelkasten_).

**Key Features**

- **Local & Private:** Your notes live on your hard drive, not on a proprietary cloud server.
    
- **Graph View:** A visual map showing how all your notes connect to one another.
    
- **Extensible:** Highly customizable with thousands of community plugins, custom CSS themes, and canvas layouts.
    
- **Future-Proof:** Notes are saved in plain Markdown text, meaning you can open them in any basic text editor even if Obsidian ceases to exist.
    

**How to Set Up Obsidian (Step-by-Step)**

1. **Download and Install**
    
    - Go to the official website ([obsidian.md](https://obsidian.md/)) and download the app for your OS (Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, or Android).
        
2. **Create Your First Vault**
    
    - Open Obsidian and click **Create new vault**.
        
    - Give your vault a name (e.g., _Personal Notes_ or _Knowledge Base_).
        
    - Choose a folder location on your computer where you want all your notes to be stored.
        
3. **Learn the Core Basics**
    
    - **Create a note:** Click the **New Note** icon (a page with a plus sign) in the top-left sidebar.
        
    - **Link notes:** Type `[[` followed by the name of another note (e.g., `[[Book Recommendations]]`). If the target note doesn't exist yet, Obsidian will create a link to a new blank note with that title.
        
    - **Use Markdown:** Format your text using standard syntax like `#` for headings, `**bold**` for bold text, and `-` for bulleted lists.
        
4. **Enable Essential Core Plugins**
    
    - Click the **Settings** gear icon in the bottom-left corner.
        
    - Select **Core plugins**.
        
    - Enable useful built-in features like **Canvas** (visual mind-mapping), **Daily notes**, and **Backlinks**.
        
5. **Customize the Look & Workflow**
    
    - **Themes:** Go to **Settings > Appearance > Themes** and click **Browse** to choose a community design.
        
    - **Community Plugins:** Go to **Settings > Community plugins**, turn off _Restricted Mode_, and click **Browse** to install popular extensions like _Dataview_ (for querying data) or _Tasks_ (for to-do tracking).

**What Prompting Is**

Prompting is the act of giving instructions, questions, or context to an AI model. It acts as the input that guides the AI to generate a specific, relevant response.

**How Prompting Works**

AI models operate by predicting the most statistically likely sequence of words based on patterns learned from vast datasets. When you provide a prompt, the AI uses your text as context to calculate and output the best step-by-step response to match your request.

**How to Build a Proper Prompt**

To get the clearest output, structure your prompt with these essential elements:

1. **Role:** Assign a clear persona or area of expertise.
    
2. **Task:** Clearly state what you want the AI to do.
    
3. **Context:** Provide background details, constraints, or goals.
    
4. **Format:** Specify how you want the output structured (e.g., bullet points, table, short paragraph).
    

**Standard Prompt Format Structure**

> **Role:** You are an expert [profession/persona].
> 
> **Task:** Please [action verb] a [deliverable].
> 
> **Context:** The target audience is [audience]. The goal is to [objective]. Include details like [specific constraints/inputs].
> 
> **Format:** Present the answer in [bullets / table / max word count / step-by-step list].

**Example of a Structured Prompt**

> **Role:** You are a senior study advisor.
> 
> **Task:** Create a 3-day revision timetable for a high school history exam.
> 
> **Context:** The student has 2 hours available each day and needs to cover WWII, the Cold War, and the Civil Rights Movement.
> 
> **Format:** Format as a Markdown table with columns for Day, Topic, Time Allocated, and Recommended Activity.
>
